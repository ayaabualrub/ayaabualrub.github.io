About

Where the data meets the dinner table

Aya's research sits at the intersection of childcare, culture, and quantitative rigor. Her work on NIH-funded projects has taken her into more than 25 rural Nebraska childcare settings, gathering dietary observations, anthropometric measurements, and Veggie Meter® readings that turn everyday feeding moments into usable evidence.

She works comfortably across methods — running multilevel mixed logistic regressions on parent-provider feeding alignment one week, leading thematic and ethnographic analysis the next. She has translated and culturally adapted the EAT Family Style intervention for Arabic-speaking families in Lincoln, and partnered with Nebraska Extension to bring it into the community.
